What the service does
---------------------

When you click "Connect and scan", and later each time you click "Scan now", we scan your store, and the plugin shows the results on its page in your store's admin: a score, the checks that cost points, the measurements and how the score changes over time. Our scanner loads your store's public pages like a visitor would and measures response times, page weight and caching, and the scan also uses data the plugin reads from your store, including your store's business context and a profile of one checkout page load on your store's server, as described in our Privacy Policy (privacy-policy.txt, shipped with the plugin). Neither the scanner nor the plugin changes anything on your store, apart from a small table of daily shop totals the plugin keeps. Nothing is emailed.

Results describe your store at the moment of each scan. Results can vary between audits with traffic, hosting and caching.

---------------------------

The scans, the results and the plugin are free. To keep the service sustainable and protect the stores whose addresses are submitted, audits have these limits:

* Each store can be scanned once a week; until then, the plugin shows the date of the next scan and its button is disabled. A scan that failed doesn't count.

* Each store can run at most 3 new scans in 24 hours, failed ones included, and 3 requests an hour from the store's server.
